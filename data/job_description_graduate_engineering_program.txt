THARA ENERGY PUBLIC COMPANY LIMITED
JOB DESCRIPTION & SUCCESS PROFILE
================================================================================

Position Title:   Graduate Engineer (Graduate Engineering Development Program)
Department:       Operations / Upstream / Downstream & Petrochemicals / New Energy
Reports To:       Department Manager / Technical Mentor
Location:         Bangkok Headquarters, Rayong Complex, and Offshore Platforms (Gulf of Thailand)
Employment Type:  Full-time, 2-Year Rotational Development Program

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Thara Energy is a leading integrated Thai oil and gas company engaged in 
exploration and production (E&P) in the Gulf of Thailand, modern refining, 
petrochemical manufacturing, and energy trading and retail. 

As the energy landscape evolves, Thara Energy is actively driving the low-carbon 
energy transition through natural gas initiatives, carbon capture and storage (CCS), 
hydrogen development, and digital technology integration. We are committed to 
building a sustainable energy future for Thailand while nurturing the next 
generation of world-class technical leaders.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROGRAM PURPOSE & OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Graduate Engineering Development Program is a structured 24-month accelerated 
leadership and technical track designed to address our long-term succession needs. 
With approximately 30% of our senior technical experts retiring over the next decade, 
this program aims to build a high-performing talent pipeline for critical future 
roles (e.g., Offshore Installation Manager, Refinery Operations Manager, Reservoir 
Engineer, Process Safety Lead).

Through structured rotations across traditional hydrocarbon operations, renewable/low-carbon 
projects, and digital innovation teams, graduates will gain cross-functional exposure, 
hands-on engineering experience, and executive mentorship.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. KEY ROTATIONS & RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
During the 2-year program, Graduate Engineers will complete 3 to 4 cross-functional 
rotations tailored to their technical discipline:

A. Core Technical Operations (Upstream / Downstream)
   - Assist in monitoring plant production, process optimization, asset integrity, 
     and safety management systems.
   - Participate in turnarounds, offshore platform operations, or refinery unit 
     de-bottlenecking projects under senior mentor guidance.
   - Conduct technical risk assessments, root-cause analyses, and equipment 
     troubleshooting.

B. Energy Transition & Low-Carbon Projects
   - Participate in feasibility studies and engineering design for natural gas, 
     Carbon Capture, Utilization, and Storage (CCUS), or hydrogen initiatives.
   - Analyze carbon emissions data and evaluate decarbonization solutions across 
     operational sites.

C. Digital & Data Engineering Innovations
   - Collaborate with data science and IT teams to implement predictive maintenance, 
     digital twin models, and IoT sensors on operational assets.
   - Utilize data analytics to improve operational efficiency and energy management.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. KEY SUCCESS CRITERIA & QUALIFICATIONS (EVALUATION MATRIX)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Candidates will be evaluated against five core criteria:

1. Educational Background & Academic Foundation
   - Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Chemical, Mechanical, Electrical, Petroleum, 
     Environmental, Instruments/Control, or Data/Digital Engineering.
   - Strong academic performance (Minimum GPA 2.80/4.00 or equivalent).

2. Technical & Digital Aptitude
   - Solid knowledge of core engineering principles relevant to the discipline.
   - Proficiency or strong interest in digital tools, data analytics (e.g., Python, 
     Power BI, SQL), process simulation, or automation.

3. Energy Transition & Sustainability Mindset
   - Passion for the energy sector, decarbonization, sustainability, and green 
     technology innovations.
   - Problem-solving skills oriented toward sustainability and resource efficiency.

4. Leadership, Agility & Communication
   - Strong adaptability, willingness to work in diverse environments (including 
     offshore and industrial plants in Rayong).
   - Excellent teamwork, project management, and analytical thinking.
   - High learning agility and readiness for accelerated career progression.

5. Language Proficiency
   - Good command of spoken and written English (TOEIC score 650+ or equivalent).
   - Fluency in Thai (required for site-level communication and operations).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT WE OFFER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Accelerated career path toward senior specialist and technical manager roles.
- Direct mentorship from industry veterans and executive leaders.
- Hands-on experience across offshore, refinery, and emerging green energy assets.
- Competitive compensation, housing allowance for site rotations, and comprehensive health benefits.
